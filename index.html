Privacy Policy — Limbo Shorts Uploader Last updated: October 5, 2026 Limbo Shorts Uploader is a personal command-line tool used by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (through the YouTube Data API v3) and to the owner's own TikTok account (through the TikTok Content Posting API). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the owner. What data the app accesses YouTube — through Google OAuth, the app requests these permissions for the owner's own Google accounts: * youtube.upload — to upload videos and set their thumbnails; * youtube.readonly — to read the name of the signed-in channel, so the owner can confirm that a video is uploaded to the correct channel. TikTok — through TikTok Login Kit, the app requests these permissions for the owner's own TikTok account: * user.info.basic — to read the account name, so the owner can confirm the correct account is used; * video.upload and video.publish — to upload and publish the owner's videos; * video.list — to list the owner's already published videos, so the owner can check which clips are already posted and avoid duplicates. The app does not access any data of other YouTube or TikTok users. How data is used and stored - Video files, titles, descriptions and tags are read from the owner's computer and sent only to YouTube or TikTok through their official APIs. - OAuth access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer and are used only to authorize these uploads. - The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use any analytics or advertising services. Revoking access - Google / YouTube: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions - TikTok: in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions (Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token files also removes the app's access on this computer. Third-party terms This app uses YouTube API Services.
